About Me

I’m a Web Design Specialist and Frontend Developer with a background in computer science and a strong focus on building thoughtful, accessible digital experiences. I grew up in India and pursued my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at Amity University, where I developed a strong foundation in programming, problem-solving, and software systems. Wanting to deepen my understanding of how people interact with technology, I moved to the U.S. to earn my Master’s degree in Computer Science at the University of Florida.

During my graduate studies, I became increasingly drawn to the intersection of design and development, especially how well-crafted interfaces can make complex systems feel intuitive and human. That interest led me to work across roles in UX design, frontend development, and web design, where I’ve translated ideas and requirements into responsive, accessible, and scalable products.

I’ve designed end-to-end user experiences, built React-based applications, and collaborated closely with cross-functional teams to turn Figma and Adobe designs into production-ready interfaces. In my current role as a Web Design Specialist with Alachua County, I build and maintain public-facing government websites, focusing on accessibility, usability, and performance. I enjoy working in environments where design decisions directly impact real people, and where clarity and inclusivity matter.

What sets me apart is my curiosity beyond just "making things look good". I’m interested in how systems work under the hood, how users actually behave, and how design and engineering can work together to create better outcomes. I’ve also had the opportunity to contribute to academic research and publications in machine learning, which continues to shape how I think about data-driven and intelligent systems.

Outside of work, I love art, cats, sunsets, and playing with color—simple things that keep me creatively energized.
